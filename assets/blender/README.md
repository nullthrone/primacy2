# PRIMACY2 Blender assets

Every model in `packages/client/public/models/` is generated by a script in this folder.
Nothing is modelled by hand, so a model is changed by editing its generator and running it again.

## Regenerate

Blender 5.2 (4.2 or newer should work), no add-ons beyond the bundled glTF exporter.

```
blender --background --python assets/blender/build_all.py                 # all registered models
blender --background --python assets/blender/build_all.py -- ship_wisp    # selected keys
blender --background --python assets/blender/build_all.py -- --no-preview ship_wisp
```

Inside a running Blender (Scripting tab or the MCP server):

```python
path = r"<repo>/assets/blender/build_all.py"
ns = {'__name__': 'build_all', '__file__': path}
exec(compile(open(path, encoding='utf-8').read(), path, 'exec'), ns)
result = ns['build'](['ship_wisp'])
```

Each build rebuilds the collection `P2_<key>` in the scene `P2_Assets` from scratch, writes
`packages/client/public/models/<key>.glb` and renders `assets/blender/previews/<key>.png`.
Objects outside the `P2_*` collections are never touched and the .blend file is not saved.
The process exits with code 1 if a model reports a problem.

## Files

| file | purpose |
|---|---|
| `p2lib.py` | palette, lofted hulls (`S`, `Loft`, `Part`, `Model`), bevels, weighted normals, AO bake, GLB export and verification, preview stage |
| `ships/kit.py` | ship building blocks (canopy, gun, turret, antenna, lamp, engine) and `finish()` |
| `ships/<key>.py` | one generator per hull with `KEY`, `RADIUS`, `HARDPOINTS` and `build()` |
| `build_all.py` | registry `MODELS` and the command line entry point |
| `previews/` | one EEVEE render per model |

## Conventions

- Metres. Blender: nose +Y, top +Z, right +X. The exporter turns that into glTF nose -Z, top +Y.
  A catalog point (x, y, z) is the Blender point (x, -z, y).
- One root empty named after the key; the mesh `<key>_mesh` and all attachment empties are its children.
  Empties: `engine_<n>` at nozzle exits, `hardpoint_<n>` exactly at the catalog positions, `light_<n>`,
  `cockpit` (pilot eye).
- Materials are plain Principled BSDF values: `hull_primary`, `hull_secondary`, `hull_dark`, `glass`,
  `emissive_engine`, `emissive_light`, `emissive_accent`, plus per-model variants
  `hull_primary_<key>` (hull colour) and `hull_accent_<key>` (class accent colour).
- Detail is geometry: lofted fuselages cut into raised panels, bevelled wings and frames, vents, strips.
  Bevels are applied in the generator (same result as an applied Bevel modifier, 2 segments).
- The mesh carries baked ambient occlusion in the vertex colour attribute `Col` (glTF `COLOR_0`).
  Pass `ao=False` to `build()` to leave it out.
- `verify_glb()` checks every export: single root, palette names, hardpoints, cockpit and engine
  empties, bounding radius within 15 % of the catalog radius, triangle budget, size below 2 MB.

## Adding a model

Copy a generator, change `KEY`, `RADIUS`, `HARDPOINTS`, and register it in `MODELS` in `build_all.py`.
Design from the hardpoints: decide which structure passes through each one, then fit the loft
stations around it. `finish()` reports the distance from every hardpoint to the nearest vertex.

## Structures and props

Generators live in `structures/` (station_ring, station_outpost, gate, beacon) and `props/`
(asteroid_0..3, debris_0..3, cargo_pod, missile, cockpit_generic); `structures/skit.py` holds
their shared helpers, `props/rockkit.py` the asteroid builder and `props/debriskit.py` the
debris finish. All are registered in `build_all.py`:

    blender --background --python assets/blender/build_all.py -- station_ring gate

- Structures are built around Blender +Z, which the exporter turns into glTF +Y, the axis used
  by the colliders in `packages/shared/src/structures.ts`. Every structure report contains a
  `fit` block (largest radius against `bound`, how far the mesh reaches into each collider and
  the largest protrusion outside them), so a changed collider shows up as a changed number.
- `dock_0` sits on the axis just outside the +Y cap of the hub or core (the caps are solid in
  the collider). Spokes, stays, solar wings, masts and antennas have no colliders.
- `gate.glb` carries two extra mesh nodes under the root: `portal` (disc r 400, double-sided
  material `portal`) and `scaffold` (construction cage), meant to be shown or hidden by the game.
  `light_0..3` are the lamps of the four anchor blocks, `light_4..11` those of the other blocks.
  Radiator panels, anchor modules and masts stand up to 15 m out of the torus collider.
- Asteroids are normalised to a bounding radius of exactly 1 m. They have one material (`rock`)
  and one primitive. Four characters: contact binary, rubble pile, shard, layered slab.
  `rockkit.build_rock` evaluates a union of chipped ellipsoid lobes on a dense icosphere,
  decimates it to the budget and adds boulders.
- Debris pieces (`debris_0` torn hull plate, `debris_1` broken truss, `debris_2` shattered engine
  bell, `debris_3` pipe cluster) are normalised to 1 m as well, kind `debris`, at most 650
  triangles and two materials each.
- On asteroids and debris `Col` is more than occlusion: it is occlusion multiplied by an albedo
  tint (rock: dust, fresh fracture faces, mineral bands; debris: soot and heat discolouration at
  the tears). It only ever darkens, so the `rock` base colour is the brightest tone. The game
  has to keep vertex colours switched on for these materials.
- `skit.finish(post=...)` runs a callback between the occlusion bake and the export;
  `skit.multiply_col` is the helper that writes such tints without splitting vertices.
- Previews multiply `Col` into the material colours, as the game does
  (`render_preview(show_col=False)` turns that off).
- `cockpit_generic` has the pilot's eye at the origin (empty `eye`), looking toward glTF -Z.
- `previews/_sheet_*.png` are contact sheets of the single previews (`skit.contact_sheet`).
