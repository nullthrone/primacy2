# Sounds for the owner to record

The owner offered to record household sounds with his phone as raw material for the sound
library. His own recordings fit the repository's licence. Given to him on 2026-11-14.

How: lay the phone directly on the device where that is possible (the library is about sound that
travels through a structure), 10 to 20 seconds each, two or three variants, in a quiet room, in
the best quality the phone offers (WAV if it can). Put the files into `assets/audio/recordings/`
with the number of the list in the name.

| No. | What | For |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Refrigerator: the compressor starts, runs, switches off | machines at idle, the cabin |
| 2 | Washing machine in the spin cycle, phone on the casing | engines felt through the hull |
| 3 | Dishwasher or washing machine: pump and valve clicks | pumps, valves |
| 4 | Extractor hood or bathroom fan: on, running, off | fans, life support |
| 5 | Computer or laptop fan from close by | the cabin at rest |
| 6 | Vacuum cleaner: start, run, run-down | turbines, the drive charging |
| 7 | Cordless screwdriver or drill: start and run-down, held to the casing | landing gear, servos |
| 8 | Shutter motor, garage door drive or electric car window | landing gear, lifts |
| 9 | Power supply, transformer, fluorescent tube or microwave oven running (the hum) | shield generator, beam weapons |
| 10 | Light switch, toggle switch, the switch of a power strip | switches |
| 11 | A knob with detents on a stove or washing machine | rotary selectors |
| 12 | Relay clicks: a car's indicator, a heating thermostat, a kettle switching off | relays |
| 13 | A heavy metal door or garage door falling shut; a car door from inside | docking clamps, hits heard from inside |
| 14 | Sitting in a car: engine start, idle, a little throttle while standing | a vehicle heard from inside |
| 15 | Fist or rubber mallet against a radiator, a metal bin, a washing machine drum | hits on the hull |
| 16 | A baking tray or pot dragged over tiles or stone | scraping over the ground |
| 17 | Sand, rice or gravel trickling onto a baking tray | regolith and debris on the hull |
| 18 | Creaking: wooden stairs, a door hinge, a metal shelf under load | the hull under stress, the ship settling |
| 19 | Hissing: a bicycle valve, an air pump, steam from an espresso machine | thrusters |
| 20 | Metal cooling down: an oven or an exhaust after use (the ticking) | thermal ticks |
